Camp Eastman is on Lake Shore Boulevard in Irondequoit. The park has six buildings you can rent. The two larger ones are Kusak Lodge and the Spies Conference Center. The four cabins are Rotary, Veterans Memorial, Eastridge Kiwanis, and Ontario. A spray park sits at the back, near Kusak Lodge, with six water features and a shelter with picnic tables. One pavilion and one gazebo there are open to all, and you cannot reserve them. The park also has athletic fields, a playground, and walking trails. Open fires and portable grills are not allowed.
