Howell Road Park is 208.6 acres of open fields and woods on both sides of Howell Road in the northeast part of Perinton. Houses surround the park. Sections of the Crescent Trail go through it, and you can cross-country ski in winter. The town is building a disc golf course in the east part of the park with Greater Rochester Disc Golf. The parking area is a gravel lot on the east side of the park. Some trails cross private land, so stay on the marked trail. Bikes are not allowed on the trails. Keep dogs on a leash and pick up after them. The town page lists hunting as an allowed use.
