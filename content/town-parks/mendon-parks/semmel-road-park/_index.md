Semmel Road Park is a Town of Mendon park on Semmel Road, about 0.37 miles east of Quaker Meeting House Road. It has a softball and baseball field, two tennis courts, a basketball court, and a children's playground. You can also walk its trails or skate on its ice rink. Restrooms and a drinking fountain are here too.
