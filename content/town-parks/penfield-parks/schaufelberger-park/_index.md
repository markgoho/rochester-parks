Schaufelberger Park is a small pocket park at the Historic Four Corners, where Route 441 meets Five Mile Line Road. Its main feature is a bronze statue of Daniel Penfield, the town's founder. The statue was commissioned for Penfield's 2010 bicentennial and went up in 2012. Don Sottile is the sculptor.
