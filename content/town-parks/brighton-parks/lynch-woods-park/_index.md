Lynch Woods Nature Park sits east of the Lehigh Valley Trail. It runs between Crittenden Road and Brighton-Henrietta Town Line Road. A 2010 survey found forest and wetlands along Crittenden Creek, home to beavers, great blue herons, and pileated woodpeckers. About 55 acres of the site are wetlands. The Town's 2010 plan for the park called for stone-dust trails and boardwalks across the wet areas. It also called for a parking lot and a trailhead kiosk.
