Town Hall Park is next to the Rush Town Hall at 5977 East Henrietta Road. It has a playground, a basketball court and a gaga pit. There are also sports fields and walking trails along Honeoye Creek. The park has a pavilion that you can rent. The Recreation Department manages the rentals. Check the Pavilion Rental Calendar on the Town website, then send the Pavilion Reservation Form. Staff will contact you to confirm the time. You can rent it for a full day, 9am to 8pm, or a half day: 9am to 12pm, 2pm to 6pm, or 3pm to 8pm. Call the Recreation Department at 585-533-2340.
