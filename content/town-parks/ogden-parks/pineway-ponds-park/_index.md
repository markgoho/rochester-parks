Pineway Ponds Park is the Town of Ogden's largest park, with more than 75 acres north of the canal near the Village of Spencerport. It has seven playfields, three hard-surface courts, and a playground with a splash pad. Four lodges in the park hold events and meetings. Walking paths run along the edges of the fields, and you can fish in the ponds with a license. About 250 parking spots serve the park.
