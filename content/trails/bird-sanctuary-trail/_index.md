Bird Sanctuary Trail is a Town of Webster trail along Mill Creek, with a pond and spots for birdwatching. It runs 0.8 miles, linking the walking path at Charles E. Sexton Memorial Park to State Route 250. From there it connects to the Chiyoda Trail and the Hojack Trail, part of more than 20 miles of off-road trails in Webster. Parking is on Orchard Street, at the trailhead or at the nearby Sexton Park lot.
