Union Station Park has sports fields, nature trails, a fitness court, and ADA-accessible paved paths. You can launch a kayak here onto Black Creek, and the park has a kick wall. A rental lodge serves groups and events. In 2024, the town added new rubber surfacing to the playground. A Little Free Library, built by local Boy Scouts, is in the park. The town opened a sprayground here in 2015.
