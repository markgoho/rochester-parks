Egypt Park sits at Pittsford-Palmyra Road and Victor Road in southeast Perinton. The Humane Society at Lollypop Farm operates the park under a lease with the town. It has a playground, a shelter, and a multi-use field, plus grills and picnic tables. The RS&E Trolley Trail connects right to the park, so you can walk or bike in. A wetland near the parking lot gives visiting families a chance to see nature up close. It is one of the town's most used parks.
