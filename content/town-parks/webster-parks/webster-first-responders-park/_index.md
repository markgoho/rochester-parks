First Responders Park is at 1350 Chiyoda Drive, with parking west of the Recreation Center. It is dedicated to Webster's first responders. The park has a spray park, a playground, tennis and pickleball courts, and a basketball court. You will also find picnic tables, a grill, and a pavilion you can reserve first come, first served, plus a walking and biking path.
