Lookup Park is at 3850 East Henrietta Road in Henrietta. It has a 9-hole disc golf course, a golf driving range, and a dog park. The Henrietta Foundation runs the driving range. The dog park is self-monitored. It is part of the Monroe County Dog Park system, along with Ellison Park, Greece Canal Park, Pittsford Dog Park, and Sweden Park. The park also has the Baldwin Cabin.
