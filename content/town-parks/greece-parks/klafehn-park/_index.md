Klafehn Park is 73.64 acres of town land at 3688 Latta Road, north of Latta Road and east of Manitou Road. The town's 2013 trail map shows nature trails here. The town's 2001 plan listed the park as undeveloped and said to wait to build fields and a pavilion until sewers reach the area.

Next door, at 3666 Latta Road, the North Greece Fire District owns and runs Firemen's Field and its pavilion. The pavilion seats 150 inside, with 12 picnic tables outside in summer. Call (585) 392-7083 to rent it.
