Rochester NY is an absolutely amazing place to live, work, and play. Within a span of less than 30 miles, one can see farms, lakes, a river, waterfalls, the beach, and so much more. The diversity of both landscape and lifestyle is a treasure with which most Rochesterians are well-acquainted. Parks are a valuable asset in this landscape!

Most of us know what park is down the street. It's the one that you rented for a birthday party, or where you play foursquare with the guys. Each park is unique–some are really amazing and some really don't even deserve to be called parks.

On this site, you'll find reviews, photos, videos, and other cool stuff all about the parks in Rochester NY. There are 19 towns in Monroe County that support over *one hundred* parks. The City of Rochester boasts over *one hundred* parks within its borders! The Monroe County park system has 23 parks.
