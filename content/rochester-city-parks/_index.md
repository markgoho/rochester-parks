Here you will find a map of both the neighborhoods of Rochester NY and the parks contained within. The neighborhood boundaries were sourced online and include a subjective description of each area. The views expressed in the map do not necessarily reflect the opinions of Rochester Parks, or (for that matter) reality.

You are welcomed to click around the map, read the descriptions, and also click on any of the markers to find out more about a particular park. Please note: the **green** markers have a full write-up while the **red** markers are, as yet, unfinished.
