Horizon Hill is 73.5 acres of natural open space off Garnsey Road in the southwest part of Perinton. It is a favorite place for hikers. Several trails go through it, including sections of the Crescent Trail. The town keeps maintenance light so the natural setting stays as it is. There are no buildings or other facilities. Some trails cross private land, so stay on the marked path. Bikes are not allowed on the trails. Keep dogs on a leash and pick up after them.
