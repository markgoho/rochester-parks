Evergreen Tot Lot is a small playground at 211 Evergreen Road in Brockport. The Village of Brockport lists it as Evergreen Park. It covers about three-tenths of an acre and is built mainly for younger children. Equipment includes swings, a slide, two balance beams, a spring seesaw, and a turbo tower climber. Two benches and a bike rack sit nearby. Eagle Scout Jay Thomas built a paved path and wooden steps with handrails. They lead up to the Erie Canal.
