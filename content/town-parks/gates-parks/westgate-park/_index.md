Westgate Park covers 16.5 acres at 1489 Howard Road, next to the post office. You can play on a lighted tennis court, two pickleball courts, or four basketball courts. The park also has two baseball fields, two playgrounds, and a nature trail. An enclosed shelter with a covered patio sits near the fields.
