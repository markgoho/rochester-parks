Breese Park sits at 108 Westcombe Park in Henrietta, along the Genesee River. An accessible stone-dust trail runs beside the river. The property has bluebell and trillium flowers, ferns, mushrooms, and black walnut trees. Eagle Scout volunteers built a pedestrian bridge, a kiosk, and a wildlife observation blind here. The Breese Park Play and Fitness trail opened in November 2021.
