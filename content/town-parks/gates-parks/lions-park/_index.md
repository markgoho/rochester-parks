Lions Park covers 14 acres at 100 Kentucky Avenue, off Long Pond Road. You can play on three lighted tennis courts, a lighted basketball court, or a sand volleyball court. The park also has a soccer and football field and a playground. An enclosed shelter with a covered patio stands nearby. It is heated but has no air conditioning.
