Copper Beech Park is in Pittsford, at 14 State Street and 10 North Main Street. It covers under a quarter acre. The park is built around an old copper beech tree, and a sapling cloned from that tree grows nearby. You can sit at the plaza patio, under a shade pergola, or at game tables set up for play.
