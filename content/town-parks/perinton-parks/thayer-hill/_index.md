Thayer Hill is 68 acres of open space off Pittsford-Palmyra Road (Route 31) in the southeast part of Perinton. It opened in 1994. Hiking trails go through woods that are growing back on their own. The Crescent Trail goes through it and joins Indian Hill nearby. It is a popular place to watch birds. The trails here are some of the only trails in Perinton that horses use, but the town does not officially open them to horses. There are no buildings or other facilities.
