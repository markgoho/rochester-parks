Whiting Road Nature Preserve covers 240 acres at 403 Whiting Road. Seven miles of color-marked trails cross woods, fields, and wetlands. They link to the neighboring Gosnell Big Woods and Herman Road Forever Wild Forest preserves. You can walk, run, bike, ride a horse, watch birds, snowshoe, or ski here. Leashed dogs are welcome. The preserve is an important stop for birds along Lake Ontario. Fires, motorized vehicles, and camping are not allowed. From October through December, archery hunters enrolled in the town program may hunt here.
