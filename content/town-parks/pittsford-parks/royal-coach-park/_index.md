Royal Coach Park is open space on West Bloomfield Road in Pittsford. A natural-surface trail, the Royal Coach Loop, runs 1.26 miles through the property. The Buholtz and Barker families once farmed this land. Stay on the path, clean up after your dog, and leave horses and motorized vehicles at home. Alcohol and hunting are not allowed.
