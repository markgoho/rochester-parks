Frisbee Hill Park sits on land that once held a town landfill. Workers closed and capped the landfill in the early 1990s. A small historic cemetery lies in the western part of the park. In winter, people go sledding on the park's hill. A natural pond forms near the base of the hill each winter, and it freezes over for ice skating. The park also has a playground, picnic areas, and open green space.
