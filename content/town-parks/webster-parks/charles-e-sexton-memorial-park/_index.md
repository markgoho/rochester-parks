Charles E. Sexton Memorial Park is off Route 104, near Route 250 on Holt Road. It was once called North Ponds Park, then renamed to honor Charles E. Sexton. A paved path about a mile long circles the park, with fitness stations along the way. You can fish, play disc golf, or picnic under a pavilion. The park has a lodge that holds up to 35 people, with a kitchen, a refrigerator, a microwave, and a bathroom.
