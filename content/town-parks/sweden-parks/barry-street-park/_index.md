Barry Street Park sits between Barry Street and Lyman Street, northeast of the Erie Canal in Brockport. The park covers 1.5 acres and has open space for activities such as recreational football. It has the only basketball court among the village's nine parks, plus two pickleball courts added in 2014. The Brockport Integrated Service and Community Organization added playground equipment in 1999. A parking lot, sidewalk, and boundary fence went in during 2007. The next year brought toddler swings, benches, and a PetSmart-donated pavilion with an ADA-compliant picnic table. It also added a climbing tunnel and better lighting. The Village Tree Board planted new trees in the park on Arbor Day 2012.
