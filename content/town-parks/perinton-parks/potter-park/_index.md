Potter Park is a Village of Fairport park on West Church Street, run by the Town of Perinton under a lease with the village. It is a neighborhood park that residents of nearby village streets can easily reach. The park holds the Lion's Den, a teen center that can also be rented for gatherings and events. Its community garden started in 2011 on the site of a World War II victory garden, and garden members donate extra produce to local food pantries. The park also has basketball, pickleball, and tennis courts, a ball field, and a playground.
