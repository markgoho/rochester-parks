The Paul M. Spiegel Pittsford Community Center sits on 4.2 acres at 35 Lincoln Avenue in the Village of Pittsford. The building holds the town Recreation Department, the Senior Center and the VanHuysen Community Room. It also runs Child Watch, after-school and summer camp programs. The building is open Monday to Thursday 8am to 9pm, Friday and Saturday 8am to 8pm, and Sunday noon to 5pm. Outside there is a new playground, picnic tables and an open lawn. Fires are not allowed. All Pittsford town parks close at dusk.
