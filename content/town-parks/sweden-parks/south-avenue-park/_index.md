South Avenue Park sits on about one acre at 52 South Avenue in Brockport. It lies in the southeastern part of the village. A large beech tree there has stood for about 125 years. The land was once the site of the Dailey homestead. The village bought the land in 1997 and made it an official park in the fall of 2006. People use the open green space for games and picnics.
