Monroe Street Village Park is a Village of Honeoye Falls park on Monroe Street, on the western edge of the village. It has two softball fields, a soccer field, and a skateboard area. Trails lead to Honeoye Creek, which runs behind the park. Men's and women's softball teams and Honeoye Falls Youth Soccer Leagues use the park almost daily in summer. The village added public restrooms in 2007 with a Monroe County grant. A 2009 study looked at extending the Lehigh Valley Trail from Great Bend Park in Mendon through this park to the village center.
