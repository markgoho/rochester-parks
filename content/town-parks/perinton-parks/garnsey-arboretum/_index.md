Garnsey Road Arboretum is a wooded park off Garnsey Road in southwest Perinton. Its low, flood-prone ground limits what the town can build there, so it stays mostly natural. It is not built for active recreation, but you can hike its trails, watch birds, or walk through its plant collection. A multi-use field also sits on the property. The park is not highly visible from the road and sees little traffic.
