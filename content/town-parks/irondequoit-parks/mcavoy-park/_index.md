McAvoy Park sits at the corner of Winton Road and Empire Boulevard. It has two softball fields, one field for football, soccer, field hockey, and lacrosse, and one medium-size field. All the fields are lighted and have synthetic turf. The park also has a playground, a snack bar, and restrooms.
