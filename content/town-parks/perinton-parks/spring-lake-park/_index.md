Spring Lake Park sits behind Whitney Road West in northwest Perinton, along Irondequoit Creek. You can fish the creek, walk its trails, or use the multi-use field. The park has a playground, a softball field, picnic tables, grills, and restrooms. A trail connects Spring Lake Park to Channing Philbrick Park in the Town of Penfield.
