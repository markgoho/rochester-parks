Vest Pocket Park is a small Village of Honeoye Falls park on West Main Street, across from Norton Street. It holds the Honeoye Falls Totem Pole. The park has picnic tables, benches, and a view of Honeoye Creek. Residents use it to eat lunch in the summer.
