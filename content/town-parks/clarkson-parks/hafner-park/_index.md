Hafner Park sits on Lake Road in Clarkson. It has a playground, two pavilions, and fields for soccer, football, and lacrosse. The park has a pond for fishing, and another for skating in winter. There is also a horse arena, outdoor basketball courts, and a tennis court. A Veterans Memorial went in during 2026. In 2023, the state gave Clarkson a grant to plan a redesign of the park. Town leaders picked Hafner Park first because they want it to become the center of a future town campus.
