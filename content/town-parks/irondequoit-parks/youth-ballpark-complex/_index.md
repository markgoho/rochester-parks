The Youth Ballpark Complex is a Town of Irondequoit park at 117 Kings Highway North. It sits directly north of Irondequoit Town Hall, behind the town hall and the library. The town lists it with its parks and facilities for its baseball fields. The fields are used for youth baseball. Teams and groups that want to use a field apply to Irondequoit Recreation with the town's field use application.
