Genesee Gateway Park sits at Mount Hope Avenue and Gregory Street, along the river between the Ford Street Bridge and downtown Rochester. The Genesee Riverway Trail runs through the park. It connects south to Genesee Valley Park, and north to the Roc City Skatepark and downtown. The park has a playground, a full-court basketball court, picnic areas, and a car-top boat launch for canoes and kayaks.
