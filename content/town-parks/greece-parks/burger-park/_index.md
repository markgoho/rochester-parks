Burger Park is a Town of Greece park on the south bank of Salmon Creek, west of the Lake Ontario State Parkway. The way in is an access drive that runs north from Hogan Point Road to a parking lot. The same drive leads to a car-top boat launch and fishing access on the creek. A second trailhead is on Hogan Point Road farther west. The Burger Park Nature Trail is about 2.9 miles of trail. It loops through fields and woods along the creek and over to the edge of the Parkway. The town's trail map shows a Wegmans Passport marker on the trail. The town asks walkers to report fallen limbs or other blockages on the trails to Parks and Recreation at (585) 723-2425.
