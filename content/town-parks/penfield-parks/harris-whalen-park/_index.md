Harris Whalen Park spreads across 45 acres off Route 441. You can play basketball or tennis on the courts, or try bocce and softball. A nature trail and hiking trails wind through the park, and in winter you can cross-country ski. The park has a playground for kids, public restrooms, charcoal grills, an open pavilion, and an enclosed lodge.
