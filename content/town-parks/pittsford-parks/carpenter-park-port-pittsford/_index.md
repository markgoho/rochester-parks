Carpenter Park sits at the Port of Pittsford, at 22 North Main Street, on the [Erie Canal](/trails/erie-canal-trail/) in Pittsford. The park covers about 2 acres. It has benches, picnic tables, and walkways along the water. A boat dock offers electric hookups and water. An outdoor stage with power hosts events. The upper part of the park holds the Pittsford Veterans Monument.
