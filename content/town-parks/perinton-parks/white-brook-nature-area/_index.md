White Brook Nature Area is in the southeast corner of Perinton. The main entrance and parking are off Aldrich Road. Trails go through more than 100 acres of fields, woods and stream land, and the Crescent Trail passes through. In winter you can cross-country ski on the trails. The Nature Center building is used for nature programs for children, and the public can rent it for events. There are shelters with grills and restrooms. You can reserve the Nature Center or a shelter through the town's WebTrac system. The town page lists hunting as an allowed use.
