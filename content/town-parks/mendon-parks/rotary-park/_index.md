Rotary Park is a Village of Honeoye Falls park on North Main Street, just north of the cemetery. It was known as North Main Street Park until 2007. That year the village and the Honeoye Falls-Mendon Rotary Club built a pavilion there and renamed it Rotary Park. Since then the village has added parking, restrooms, and playground equipment, and rebuilt the soccer fields. The park has two soccer fields and a pavilion.
