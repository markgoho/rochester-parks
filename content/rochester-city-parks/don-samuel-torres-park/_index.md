Don Samuel Torres Park sits off North Clinton Avenue at 70 Oakman Street. Families and students from the nearby School No. 20 use the park. In 2021 the city finished a renovation there. It is part of the El Camino Revitalization Plan, a partnership between the city and the Ibero-American Development Corporation to redesign northeast Rochester. The renovation added basketball courts, a baseball field with dugouts and a home run fence, and a playground with seating for parents and grandparents. Workers also added new gates and fencing, fitness equipment, and a dominoes table.
