Clarkson Recreation Park covers about 50 acres on Ridge Road. The town used to call it Ridgeway Park. It has a playground, woods, and three ball diamonds for softball. Many people walk the park's perimeter for exercise, often with a dog. The park's lodge sat closed for about ten years until the town's Highway Department rebuilt it in 2012. The new lodge seats up to 166 people. In 2026, the town added a multigolf course.
