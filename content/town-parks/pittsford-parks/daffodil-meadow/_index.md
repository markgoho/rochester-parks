Daffodil Meadow is a Town of Pittsford park between Thornell Road and Park Road. It sits in the woods on the east side of Irondequoit Creek. A town trail along the creek runs past it. In spring the meadow floor fills with daffodils, and a wooden sign marks the spot. The town's page gives no street address, parking or other amenities for it. All Pittsford town parks close at dusk.
