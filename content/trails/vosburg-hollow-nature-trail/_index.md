Vosburg Hollow Nature Trail is a short Town of Webster trail. Park at 595 Vosburg Road, then walk an eighth of a mile along the Hojack Trail to reach it. It runs 0.25 miles, for walking, hiking, jogging, and snowshoeing. Monroe County's own parcel record for that address names it the Vosburg Hollow Nature Preserve. The record also shows the land as wholly tax-exempt Town of Webster property.
