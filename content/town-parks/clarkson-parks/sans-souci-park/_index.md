Sans Souci Park sits on the Erie Canal in Clarkson, at 4159 County Line Road. Its name is French for 'without worry.' Clarkson's own highway crew built the park and its pavilion. The park has a boat launch, two docks, and a playground added in 2026. Walkers and bike riders on the canal path stop here, and so do people who come to picnic or relax. The park may have one of the smallest frontages on the canal, but it fits in a lot, including plenty of parking.
