Barnard Park began in 1937. That year the Barnard Exempt Fireman's Association bought a 16-acre tract on Maiden Lane. The group wanted to build a clubhouse and make it "the finest town small park in the state." They laid out a baseball diamond. They set up picnic tables, benches, and fireplaces for cookouts. The park and its clubhouse have hosted thousands of events over the years. The park also has a shelter with picnic tables, a playground, and a grill.
