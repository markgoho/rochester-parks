Great Bend Park is a Town of Mendon park on State Route 65 (Clover Street), about 0.43 miles south of Stoney Lonesome Road. It has walking trails. A 2009 study looked at extending the Lehigh Valley Trail from this park through Monroe Street Village Park to the village of Honeoye Falls.
