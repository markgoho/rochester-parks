Don Samuel Torres Park sits off North Clinton Avenue at 70 Oakman Street. Families and students from the nearby School No. 20 use the park. The city rebuilt the park in 2021. The work was part of the El Camino plan, which the city runs with the Ibero-American Development Corporation to remake northeast Rochester. The rebuild added basketball courts and a baseball field with dugouts and a home run fence. It added a playground with seats for parents and grandparents. Workers also put in new gates and fencing, fitness gear, and a dominoes table.
