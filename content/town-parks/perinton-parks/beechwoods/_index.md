Beechwoods is a 22.8-acre nature preserve off High Street Extension in the northeast part of Perinton. It is one of the smallest and oldest open space properties in the town. Nature trails go through low forest and along a stream. In winter you can cross-country ski on the trails. There are two small parking areas. There are no other buildings or facilities. Some trails near the preserve cross private land, so stay on the marked trail. Bikes are not allowed on the trails. Keep dogs on a leash and pick up after them.
