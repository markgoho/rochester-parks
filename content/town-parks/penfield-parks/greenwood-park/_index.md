Greenwood Park covers 21 acres at the intersection of Scribner Road and Embury Road. You can play baseball on the diamond, shoot hoops on the basketball courts, or play tennis and pickleball on shared courts. The park has playground equipment for kids and open fields for other games. A gravel lot off Scribner Road gives you parking.
