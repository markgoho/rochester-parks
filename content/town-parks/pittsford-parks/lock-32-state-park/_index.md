Lock 32 sits along the [Erie Canalway Trail](/trails/erie-canal-trail/) in Pittsford, near Nazareth University. The lock has a basic campsite for people traveling the canal by bike or boat. Only two campgrounds on the Erie Canal in western New York are run by the public, and this is one.
