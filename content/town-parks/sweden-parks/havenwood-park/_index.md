Havenwood Park is a small playground at 47 Havenwood Drive in Brockport. It covers about two-tenths of an acre and is built mainly for younger children. Equipment includes swings, a slide, and two balance beams. It also has a spring rider, an e-z digger, a rockblock climbing tunnel, and a turbo tower climber. Four benches sit in the park.
