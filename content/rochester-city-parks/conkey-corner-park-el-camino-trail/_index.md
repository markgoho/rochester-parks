The Genesee Land Trust created Conkey Corner Park from an old railroad right of way. The Land Trust worked with the City of Rochester, IBERO, and other community partners. The park sits at the corner of Conkey and Clifford Avenues. It has a playground, benches, and perennial gardens. The connected [El Camino Trail](/trails/el-camino-trail/) starts here.
