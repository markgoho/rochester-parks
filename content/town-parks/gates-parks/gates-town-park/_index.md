Gates Town Park offers something for everyone. It is the largest park in Gates, and it sits off Lyell Road down an unpaved road, which is enough to keep it quieter than its size would suggest.

![The pond at Gates Town Park](PhotomatixResults01-20-of-22.jpg)

## Play Area

The playground area is large. The main structure carries three slides and several ladders, and there is a tire play area beside it. There are two swing sets here: regular swings for big kids and toddler swings, plus a tire swing, which is rare enough in Monroe County to be worth the drive on its own.

![The playground structure at Gates Town Park](PhotomatixResults01-12-of-22.jpg)

## Athletic Fields

Roughly 90% of this park is athletic fields. There are four soccer fields and a baseball diamond. A 9-hole disc golf course runs the perimeter of the park, which makes Gates one of the few towns in the area with a course of its own.

## Water Features

There is a pond with a dock. The dock holds about ten people comfortably.

![Looking across the fields at Gates Town Park](PhotomatixResults01-14-of-22.jpg)

## Trails

Trails run along the edges of the park. Some of them appear to be maintained public trails that carry on into the surrounding woods.
