Harry Allen Park is a Village of Honeoye Falls park on North Main Street, in the heart of the village. A gazebo stands near the entrance, with a playground behind it. The park also holds the local History Museum, the Troop 10 Scout House, and an old schoolhouse. The Zebulon Norton Trail starts here and leads down to the Lower Falls before continuing on to Rotary Park. The park hosts community events, including outdoor movie nights.
