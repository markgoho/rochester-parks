McCoord Hannan Woods is a 29.2-acre wooded preserve between Garnsey Road and Kreag Road in the southwest part of Perinton. Most access points to its trails are on private land, and they are open to hikers only with the homeowners' permission. The woods have some of the steepest ground in Perinton. The trails are well known to hikers from Perinton and nearby towns. The town page lists no parking area and no facilities. Stay on the marked path. Bikes are not allowed on the trails. Keep dogs on a leash and pick up after them.
