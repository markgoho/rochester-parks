Parma Park is the Town of Parma's main park, on Route 259 at Hilton-Parma Corners Road. It has eight baseball fields, eight soccer fields, and four tennis courts, plus a disc golf course. The park has four pavilions, named Allardice, Burritt, Hess, and Lions. You can also play basketball or volleyball, or walk the trails.
