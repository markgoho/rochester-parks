Four Mile Creek Preserve sits on 72 acres at 160 Phillips Road. The Town of Webster bought the land in 2004 through an open space vote. Sections of Four Mile Creek and its tributary, Salt Creek, run through old growth forest, younger forest, and wetlands. Trails follow color-coded loops for walking, biking, snowshoeing, and cross-country skiing. Along the Red and Yellow trails you can spot old, abandoned cars left from years past. Leashed dogs are welcome, but motorized vehicles, fires, camping, and firearms are not allowed.
