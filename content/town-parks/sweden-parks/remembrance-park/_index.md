Remembrance Park is a small triangular park on Park Avenue, between High Street and Spring Street in Brockport. The village set the land aside as parkland when it first laid out the village, but left it unnamed for years. It later renamed the park Remembrance Park in memory of civilians who died in the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks in New York City. St. Luke's Episcopal Church donated a Royalty Flowering Crabtree for the park. A sculpture by Jennifer Hecker, called 'les Racines et Les Raisons,' was added in 2006. The park also has a Victorian bench donated by neighbors and friends.
