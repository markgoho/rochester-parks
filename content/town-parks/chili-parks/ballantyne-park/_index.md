Ballantyne Park is a small neighborhood park with open green space and a walking trail. The town added a new rubber surface to its playground in 2024. The park is next to The Arc of Monroe's Ballantyne Day Services and the DEC boat launch. The boat launch has an ADA-accessible kayak launch onto Black Creek and the Genesee River.
