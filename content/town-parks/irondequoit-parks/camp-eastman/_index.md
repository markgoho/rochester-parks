Camp Eastman is on Lake Shore Boulevard in Irondequoit. The park has six rentable buildings: Kusak Lodge, the Spies Conference Center, the Rotary Cabin, the Veterans Memorial Cabin, the Eastridge Kiwanis Cabin, and the Ontario Cabin. A spray park sits at the back, near Kusak Lodge, with six water features and a shelter with picnic tables. One pavilion and one gazebo there are open to all, and you cannot reserve them. The park also has athletic fields, a playground, and walking trails. Open fires and portable grills are not allowed.
