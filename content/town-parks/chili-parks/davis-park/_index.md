Davis Park is one of Chili's largest sports parks. You enter from Paul Road on the north side. The west entrance is on Chestnut Ridge Road. The park has four baseball and softball diamonds and seven rectangular athletic fields. Thousands of players use the fields each year. In 2024, Upper Davis and Lower Davis each got a new playground with a rubber surface. Paved, ADA-accessible paths connect the park for walking and running. The park has two open-air pavilions, the Stockton Pavilion and the Lions Club Pavilion. It also has two Little Free Libraries, built by local Boy Scouts.
